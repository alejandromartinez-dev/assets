Privacy Policy

Sum Gadgets for Jira · Last updated: 18 August 2026

Summary

Sum Gadgets for Jira does not collect, store or transmit any of your data. The app runs entirely inside Atlassian's infrastructure. No data ever leaves your Atlassian environment, and the app developer has no access to it.

What the app does with your data

When you view a gadget, the app asks the Jira REST API for the issues matching the JQL filter you configured, reads the numeric field you selected, adds the values up and displays the result. This happens in memory, each time the gadget is displayed.

  • Nothing is written to any database, log or file.
  • Nothing is cached beyond the lifetime of a single request.
  • Nothing is sent to any third party or to any server outside Atlassian.
  • The app makes no outbound network requests of any kind.

Permissions

The app requests a single Jira permission scope, read:jira-work, which allows it to read issues. It is read-only: the app cannot create, modify or delete anything in your Jira instance.

All Jira requests are made on behalf of the user viewing the gadget, using that user's own permissions. If a user cannot see a project in Jira, its issues are not included in the totals shown to them.

Gadget configuration

The settings you choose for a gadget — the JQL filter, the field to sum and the grouping — are stored by Jira as part of your dashboard configuration, inside your own Atlassian site. They are not stored by the app or by its developer.

Runs on Atlassian

This app is built on Atlassian Forge and carries the Runs on Atlassian designation, which means all of its compute and storage stay within Atlassian's infrastructure and it is covered by Atlassian's own data residency and security commitments.

Personal data

The app does not store personal data. Issue data, including assignee names, is read and rendered at display time and then discarded. No profiles, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
